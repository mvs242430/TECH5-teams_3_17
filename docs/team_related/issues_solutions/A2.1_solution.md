## 1. Architectural Pattern & Design Rationale
- **Pattern Applied:** MVC Controller & Internal Component Contract (`AssignmentEngineContract.compare_solutions` returning `models.RunComparison` DTO).
- **Why Chosen:** Component A (`app/visualization/`) must never re-implement comparison math in templates or routes. Instead, `compare_solutions` in `routes.py` queries `RunRepository` to validate solution IDs, invokes `AssignmentEngine().compare_solutions(sol1_id, sol2_id)` through the internal contract (with a clean fallback to `MockAssignmentEngine` if the solver engine raises `NotImplementedError`), enriches student names and project titles from `StudentRepository` and `ProjectRepository`, and passes the structured `RunComparison` DTO to `compare.html`.
- **Component Scope:** `app/visualization/routes.py`, `app/visualization/templates/compare.html`, `tests/test_visualization_component.py`.

---

## 2. High-Level Logic & Data Flow Walkthrough
1. **Selector Loading:** `GET /compare` fetches up to 50 recent runs via `RunRepository().list_runs(limit=50)` and flattens their solutions into `all_solutions` to populate the Solution A and Solution B dropdown selectors.
2. **Empty Query Parameter Handling:** When `sol1` or `sol2` is missing, `compare.html` renders only the dropdown selector form (`sol_a=None`, `sol_b=None`, `comparison=None`).
3. **Unknown ID Guard:** When `sol1` or `sol2` does not match any stored solution in `RunRepository().get_solution(...)`, a `danger` flash message is displayed and the user is safely redirected to `/compare` (HTTP 302) rather than raising a 500 error.
4. **Contract Delegation & Enrichment:** With two valid IDs, `engine.compare_solutions(sol1_id, sol2_id)` computes the `RunComparison` DTO (courtesy of `metrics.compare_two_solutions` from Issue `B1.3`). The route enriches `changed_assignments` with human-readable `student_name`, `project_a_title`, and `project_b_title` from the persistence repositories.
5. **View Rendering (`compare.html`):** Displays 5 KPI delta cards (Changed Student Allocations, $\Delta$ Average Skill Fit, $\Delta$ Average Preference Rank, $\Delta$ Gini (Fairness), and Rank Shifts), a Plain-Language Impact Summary box ("Improves / Gets worse / Who is affected"), trade-off findings, and the table of changed student allocations.

## 5. Check Your Understanding (Pedagogical Checkpoint)
1. *Why does `compare_solutions` call `AssignmentEngine().compare_solutions(sol1_id, sol2_id)` instead of subtracting metrics inside `routes.py`?*
   - **Defense:** To uphold strict separation of concerns between Component A (Visualization) and Component B (Assignment Engine). All analytical metric deltas and trade-off summaries are calculated in `app/engine/metrics.py` (`compare_two_solutions`) and exposed via `AssignmentEngineContract`.
2. *Why does `routes.py` enrich `student_name` and `project_a_title` / `project_b_title` after calling `engine.compare_solutions`?*
   - **Defense:** `models.Solution` stores `models.Assignment` records keyed by `student_id` and `project_id`. Keeping `metrics.compare_two_solutions` pure and independent of database repositories means it sets `student_id` and `project_id` as defaults; Component A's controller maps those IDs to human-readable display names and titles for the UI.
