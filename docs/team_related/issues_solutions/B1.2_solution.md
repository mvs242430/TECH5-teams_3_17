# Issue B1.2 Solution — Solution Indicators (The Scoreboard)

## 1. Architectural Pattern Applied & Rationale
- **Structured Analytical Aggregator / Contract DTO Shape (`app/engine/metrics.py`):**
  - `compute_solution_indicators` evaluates an allocation across the four mandatory dimensions of Section 7 of the TECH5 specification: **Feasibility**, **Efficiency**, **Fairness**, and **Capacity**.
  - Returns a deterministic nested dictionary structure consumed by `app/engine/recommendations.py` and Component A's Jinja2 templates (`run_detail.html`, `solution_detail.html`).

## 2. High-Level Logic & Data Flow Walkthrough
1. **Feasibility (`indicators["feasibility"]`):**
   - Verifies that every assignment references a known student and project, no student is assigned twice, all mandatory project skill requirements are met, every student in the cohort is assigned, project headcounts stay within `[min_capacity, max_capacity]`, and all `fixed_assignments` are respected.
   - Populates `is_feasible` (`len(violations) == 0`), `violations`, `assigned_count`, and `total_students`.
2. **Efficiency (`indicators["efficiency"]`):**
   - Aggregates `mean_skill_fit`, `total_skill_fit_sum`, and counts `unmet_soft_requirements_count` across all valid assignments.
3. **Fairness (`indicators["fairness"]`):**
   - Tallies `rank_distribution` (`rank_1`, `rank_2`, etc.) and `unranked_count` / `unranked_pct`.
   - Computes `mean_rank` over ranked assignments so unranked fallback assignments are tracked explicitly via `unranked_count` rather than distorting stated choice averages.
   - Assigns unranked allocations a penalty rank of `max_preference_rank + 1` when computing `worst_rank`, `rank_gini` (via `calculate_gini`), and `rank_variance`.
4. **Capacity (`indicators["capacity"]`):**
   - Computes per-project `assigned_count`, `min_capacity`, `max_capacity`, and `utilization_pct`, plus aggregate `total_assigned`, `total_capacity`, and `overall_utilization_pct`.

## 5. Pedagogical Checkpoint ("Check Your Understanding")
1. **How are unranked assignments treated across the fairness metrics?**
   - They are excluded from `mean_rank`, counted explicitly in `unranked_count` and `unranked_pct`, and given a penalty rank of `max_preference_rank + 1` in `worst_rank`, `rank_gini`, and `rank_variance`.
2. **Why is feasibility evaluated separately from efficiency and fairness?**
   - So decision makers can distinguish a rule-violating allocation (`is_feasible = False` with explicit `violations`) from a valid allocation that simply has trade-offs in preference or skill fit.
