# Issue A2.2.1 — Dedicated Solutions Hub Page & Integrated Decision Guide

`team-a` · A2 · Week 3 · M · Needs: A2.2, A2.4.1

**What and why:** Previously, the top navigation bar contained 6 data-input tabs (`Students`, `Projects`, `Preferences`, `Fixed Locks`, `Skills`, `Scenarios`), `Dashboard`, and `Compare`, but **no top-level destination for generated Solutions**. Users viewing `/runs/<id>`, `/solutions/<id>`, or `/recommendations/<id>` saw `Dashboard` highlighted as active in the navbar, yet clicking `Dashboard` lost their place and forced them to scroll down to a historical runs table to re-open solutions. Adding a dedicated **`Solutions`** tab (`GET /solutions`) immediately next to `Dashboard` provides a user-friendly hub for all roles (`ADMIN`, `COORDINATOR`, `STUDENT`) to inspect active Pareto alternatives, switch across runs, and view at-a-glance policy recommendations right alongside the solution cards while keeping `/recommendations/<run_id>` for full Evidence vs. Advice inspection.

**Files:** `app/visualization/routes.py`, `app/visualization/templates/solutions.html`, `app/visualization/templates/base.html`, `app/visualization/static/css/style.css`, `docs/architecture.md`, `tests/test_visualization_component.py` (new A2.2.1 tests only; zero existing tests modified), `docs/team_related/issues/A2.2.1.md`, `docs/team_related/issues_solutions/A2.2.1_solution.md`.

- Update active-tab highlighting in `base.html`: `Dashboard` is active only on `visualization_web.dashboard`; `Solutions` is active on `visualization_web.solutions_overview`, `visualization_web.view_run`, `visualization_web.view_solution`, and `visualization_web.view_recommendations`.
