# Issue A2.1.2 — Compare: preselect active alternatives and explain emptied past runs

`team-a` · A2 · Week 3 · S · Needs: A2.1, A2.1.1

**What and why:** Two usability and state-consistency gaps remain on `/compare` after Issues `A2.1` and `A2.1.1`:
1. **No preselection when usable runs exist:** Opening `/compare` from the navigation bar passes `sol_a=None` and `sol_b=None`, causing browsers to show `Alt #1` in both dropdowns with no comparison rendered. Furthermore, clicking **Compare This Solution With Another →** (`/compare?sol1=<id>`) on `solution_detail.html` discards `sol1` because `if not sol1_id or not sol2_id:` returns early with `sol_a=None` (regressing the single-parameter preselection present in the reference implementation).
2. **Silent blank dropdowns after scenario reload:** Loading a benchmark scenario calls `clear_all_cohort_data()`, cascading `DELETE` onto `assignments` while leaving historical `assignment_runs` and `solutions` rows intact. Dashboard still displays those past runs as `COMPLETED` with `3 solution(s)`, while `/compare` filters them out via `list_solution_ids_with_assignments()` and renders two completely blank `<select>` boxes with no explanation.

**Files:** `app/visualization/routes.py` (`dashboard`, `view_run`, `compare_solutions`, and private helpers), `app/visualization/templates/compare.html`, `app/visualization/templates/dashboard.html`, `app/visualization/templates/run_detail.html`, `app/visualization/templates/solutions.html`, `tests/test_visualization_component.py` (new tests only; do not modify existing tests), `docs/team_related/issues/A2.1.2.md`, `docs/team_related/issues_solutions/A2.1.2_solution.md`.

**What to build:**
- In `app/visualization/routes.py`:
  1. When `GET /compare` is opened with neither `sol1` nor `sol2` and usable solutions exist, automatically preselect two distinct alternatives (`Alt #1` and `Alt #2`) from the most recent multi-solution run (or compatible usable runs) and immediately render their side-by-side comparison. Never redirect in a loop on `GET /compare` if only one usable solution exists or if cross-run candidates have different student cohorts.
  2. When `GET /compare` is opened with a single `sol1` (or `sol2`) parameter (such as from `solution_detail.html`), validate that the solution exists and still has assignments (flashing `danger` and redirecting to `/compare` otherwise, matching `A2.1.1`), preselect a cohort-compatible distinct companion solution (`same run` first), and immediately render their comparison.
  3. Pass `has_emptied_runs` to `compare.html`, `emptied_run_ids` to `dashboard.html`, and `has_usable_solutions` to `run_detail.html`.
- In `app/visualization/templates/compare.html`:
  - When `all_solutions` is empty, replace blank dropdowns with a clear message explaining either that past runs lost their student allocations when cohort data was reloaded or that an optimization run must be launched first, with a link to Dashboard.
  - When only a single usable solution exists (`comparison` is `None` and `all_solutions|length == 1`), explain that at least two comparable alternatives are needed.
- In `app/visualization/templates/dashboard.html`, `app/visualization/templates/solutions.html`, and `app/visualization/templates/run_detail.html`:
  - Indicate when a past run's student allocations were cleared by a cohort reload, and add a direct **Compare Alt #1 vs Alt #2 →** link on `solutions.html` / `run_detail.html` when a run has at least two usable solutions.

**Done when:** ☐ `GET /compare` with an active run preselects `Alt #1` vs `Alt #2` and renders their comparison immediately ☐ `GET /compare?sol1=<id>` preselects `sol1` plus a distinct companion solution and renders their comparison (and flashes/redirects if `sol1` is unknown or emptied) ☐ `GET /compare` after a scenario reload with no new run explains why past runs cannot be compared instead of showing blank `<select>` boxes ☐ `dashboard.html` and the unified run/solutions view (`solutions.html` / `run_detail.html`) reflect cleared allocations and link directly to `Compare Alt #1 vs Alt #2` ☐ new tests pass and all existing tests remain untouched and passing.
